# Route Readiness

`RouteReadinessProfile` is derived from Catalog content and benchmark evidence, never
from a route-name condition. Product READY requires all of:

- complete Region, Theme, Route, and Anchor references;
- valid mandatory verified POI identity and usable planning pipeline;
- production-eligible Knowledge with complete evidence and zero hard leakage;
- verified grounded Story structure with hard metrics passing;
- verified safe Experience structure with hard metrics passing;
- persisted snapshot identity evidence;
- Product renderability and route-scoped golden evaluation cases.

Resources are an optional enhancement capability. If a route declares Resources,
their identity, truthfulness, sponsorship, feasibility, and snapshot checks must pass.
A route without commerce can still be READY when the four core cultural capabilities
pass.

The current evaluator yields the audited Jingwei route as READY. The other three
routes are COMING_SOON because their actual capability and benchmark prerequisites
are absent, not because their names differ.

## M8 Route Template

Before another route can become READY, provide and validate:

1. Region, Theme, Route, and Anchor Catalog records.
2. A verified Provider-neutral POI binding for every mandatory Anchor.
3. Reviewed Knowledge Sources, production Claims, and complete supporting Evidence.
4. A verified Story Blueprint whose Claims and POI identities pass grounding gates.
5. A verified Experience Blueprint whose activities pass observation and safety gates.
6. Route-scoped positive, negative, adversarial, and golden evaluation cases.
7. Snapshot persistence and Product capability projection.
8. Resource checks only when the route declares that optional capability.

Only after the offline benchmark and readiness evaluator pass may Product availability
be promoted to READY.
