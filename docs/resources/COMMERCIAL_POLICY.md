# Commercial Policy

Editorial recommendation and commercial relationship are independent.

`CommercialRelationship` supports `none`, `public_resource`, `partner`, `sponsored`, and `unknown`. Partner and sponsored resources require both `disclosure_required=true` and non-empty disclosure text. Any user-visible recommendation carries that disclosure explicitly.

Commercial metadata cannot:

- change a Resource fact or verification status;
- improve editorial score or rank;
- create or modify Knowledge, Story, or Experience content;
- turn a resource into a mandatory Anchor or commerce stop;
- hide unknown price, opening, or availability data;
- imply cultural identity from proximity or sponsorship.

M5 has no payment, order, inventory, coupon, commission, bidding, advertisement, or merchant-administration system. No partner or sponsored Resource was fabricated for the Jingwei package; real runtime candidates retain `commercial_relationship=unknown`.
