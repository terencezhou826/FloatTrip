# Experience Safety Contract

## Layered Enforcement

Safety is enforced twice:

1. Raw LLM output passes Context fact, Citation, qualifier, Claim-type, text-safety, structured observation, stable identity, and current-presence Evidence checks.
2. Rendered output must deterministically expose the reviewed target and hard safety constraints, then passes the same text-safety scan.

The renderer does not bypass Claim policy or sanitize unsafe model prose. An unsafe raw instruction fails even when trusted safety wording is correct.

## Observable Reality

`verified_entity` requires stable IDs; a name alone fails. `visitor_selected_visible_object` does not assert that a specific species, relic, historical object, or material exists. It requires an already visible visitor-selected object, observe-only interaction, normal visitor areas, guardian supervision when required, and a safe skip option.

`specific_current_observable` requires production-eligible supporting Claims with verified Evidence metadata `current_presence_verified=true`. Ancient texts and mythology can support narrative statements but cannot establish present-day existence.

## Prohibited Output

Deterministic scanning rejects unnegated instructions involving unsafe access, environmental removal, cultural-property contact, unsupervised children, forced purchase, restricted areas, water or road hazards, and wildlife interaction.

Hard safety visibility cannot depend on whether the LLM remembers to repeat a Prompt. Guardian, no-touch, no-move, no-collect, no-remove, barrier, normal-area, and skippable requirements are rendered from trusted structure.
