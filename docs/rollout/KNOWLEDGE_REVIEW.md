# Knowledge Review

Every production Claim requires a real Source, supporting Evidence, a precise
locator, a quote excerpt, a valid verification status, and a promotion policy whose
wording does not exceed the Evidence.

Source priority is ancient primary text, government, local chronicle, official
heritage record, museum, scenic official material, and peer-reviewed scholarship.
Self-media, ordinary travel guides, SEO aggregation, AI summaries, and copied
encyclopedia text cannot be core verified Evidence.

Mythology, local legend, official narrative, historical fact, geographic fact,
heritage fact, and academic interpretation must remain distinct. Place-associated
legends need explicit attribution such as local-tradition or official-source
qualifiers. Disputed interpretations remain disputed. `review_required` and
`internal_only` Claims never enter production Story or Experience generation.
