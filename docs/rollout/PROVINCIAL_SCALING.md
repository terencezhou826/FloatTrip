# Provincial Scaling

The replication boundary is Region, Theme, Route, Anchor, Knowledge, Story,
Experience, and route-scoped evaluation data. City and route identities are content,
not program control flow.

Province-scale rollout requires stable Region trees, package isolation, globally
unique IDs, Provider-neutral identities, package-scoped repository queries, and
cross-route leakage checks. Moving or versioning one route must not require editing
another route's content.

Production scans must reject city- or route-specific conditional branches in
Planning, Runtime, generation, Product, and readiness code. New municipalities may
add Catalog packages and fixtures only after their own identity and evidence review;
the current rollout does not authorize content outside its named package.
