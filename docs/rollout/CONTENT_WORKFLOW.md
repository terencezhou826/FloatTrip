# Content Workflow

A route moves from COMING_SOON toward READY in this order:

1. Audit existing Region, Theme, Route, Anchor, and stable IDs.
2. Discover Provider POI candidates using the Anchor and Region hierarchy.
3. Stop for human review if identity or parent/child boundaries are ambiguous.
4. Research primary texts and authoritative public sources.
5. Create narrowly scoped Sources, Claims, Evidence, locators, excerpts, and
   promotion policies.
6. Verify 100% Evidence coverage and zero production-ineligible leakage.
7. Design a route-specific Story structure from the evidence, not another route's
   chapter template.
8. Design safe Activities from that Story and audience needs.
9. Run real Planning, generation, binding, persistence, and snapshot recovery.
10. Add a route-scoped golden fixture and rerun the unchanged hard metrics.
11. Let `RouteReadinessEvaluator` determine Product availability.

Resources are optional. Missing commerce, prices, opening hours, AR, video, or GPS
sub-points must not be replaced with fabricated data and does not block core cultural
READY.
